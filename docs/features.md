# Dashboard Feature Descriptions

## 1. Sales & Profit Trend (Line Chart)
- Displays monthly sales and profit trends
- Helps identify peak and low-performing months
- Useful for understanding seasonality in e-commerce sales

## 2. Category vs. Profitability (Bar Chart)
- Compares product categories based on sales and profit
- Identifies which categories drive revenue vs. which are most profitable

## 3. Heatmap: Category vs. Month
- Visualizes how each category performs across different months
- Helps detect seasonal products or categories with fluctuating demand

## 4. KPI Summary Cards
- Displays total sales, total profit, and number of orders
- Provides at-a-glance metrics for quick decision-making

## 5. Interactive Filters
- Allows filtering by year, month, or product category
- Enables deeper exploration of specific segments
